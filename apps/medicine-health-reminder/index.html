Medicine & Health Reminder

Official application verification, privacy disclosures, and user support portal for the Medicine & Health Reminder Android application.

Application Purpose

Medicine & Health Reminder is a completely free Android application engineered to help individuals track their daily medication schedules, record vital health logs, and receive timely health reminders.

Designed with privacy-first principles, the application operates locally on your device with optional secure cloud integration.

Google OAuth & Data Scope Disclosure

To provide seamless backup and cross-device synchronization, Medicine & Health Reminder integrates with Google Drive APIs using the restricted Google Drive AppData folder scope (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata).

How this scope is used:

  • Private Application Storage: Access is strictly restricted to a hidden app data folder managed by the app, completely invisible to other applications.
  • Backup & Restore: Used exclusively to back up and restore personal medication logs and schedule preferences.
  • Data Privacy: User data is never shared, monetized, or used for tracking or advertising purposes.

Privacy Policy

All health records and medication schedules remain strictly confidential. You retain full control and can revoke application access at any time through your Google Account Permissions.
