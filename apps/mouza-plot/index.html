What it does

From Mouza sheet to plot area

Everything a landowner or buyer needs to check a plot's size on the map, in the units used for land in Bangladesh.

🛰️

Measure on the Google Map

Measure land on Google's satellite map too and compare it with the Mouza map. Opens at your location (optional); mark corners with the crosshair, by tapping, or by walking the boundary with GPS points.

🗺️

Browse Mouza Maps

Scanned Mouza maps by division › district › upazila › survey (CS, SA, RS, BS). A filter box finds a mouza quickly, in Bengali or English digits.

👁️

Preview Before You Download

Open any map to see the whole sheet first, zoom in full screen, and step through the folder with Previous / Next. Download only the one you need.

⬇️

Download One Map or a Whole Folder

Maps are saved in your Downloads folder and open without internet. Big folder downloads continue in the background; maps you already have are skipped.

📐

Measure Any Plot

Pan the map under a fixed red crosshair and add each corner, so your finger never hides the line you are tracing. Corners snap to plots you have already drawn.

🧮

Bangladeshi Land Units

Area in decimal (শতক), square feet, square metres, acre, katha and bigha, with an adjustable katha size. Side lengths on the map, perimeter in feet, metres and chains.

🎯

Every Mouza Map Scale

16, 32, 64 or 80 inches = 1 mile: pick the scale printed on the map, at the scan's 400 DPI, or mark two ends of the chain scale bar. The app warns you if the two disagree.

📄

PDF Reports & DXF

Share a PDF report of your plots with their areas and side lengths by WhatsApp or e-mail. Export to DXF to open the plots in AutoCAD.

🇧🇩

English and বাংলা

The whole app, including numbers, in English or Bengali. Also opens your own scanned maps: PDF, JPG or PNG.

How it works

Three steps to a plot's area

1

Find the map

Browse to your mouza, preview the sheet, and download it. Or open a scanned map you already have.

2

Set the scale and trace

Confirm the map scale, zoom in on your plot, and add a point at each corner with the crosshair.

3

Read the area and share

See the area in decimal, katha and bigha as you go. Name the plot by its dag number and share a PDF report.

Privacy

No account, no tracking

Mouza Plot collects nothing about you. Your maps, plots and measurements stay on your phone. The app goes online to list and download maps and to show the Google map, and it never sends your plots or your location to us.

Read Full Privacy Policy →
  • No account, no sign-in, no registration
  • No analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting
  • Plots, dag numbers and settings stay on your phone
  • Measuring works without internet
  • Location is optional, used only on your phone for the Google map tab
  • Never asks for contacts, camera or microphone
  • Reports are created only when you ask, and go only where you send them
Permissions

What the app asks for, and why

Location only if you allow it, for the Google map tab. No contacts, no camera, no microphone, no SMS, and no broad storage access on current Android versions.

📍
Location (optional)
For the Google map tab: shows where you are and adds GPS points while you walk a boundary. Read on the phone only, never sent to us. The tab works without it.
🌐
Internet & Network State
To list and download Mouza maps, and to check for Wi-Fi before a large download. Measuring works offline.
🔔
Notifications
To show download progress and completion. Optional: downloads still work if you refuse.
⏳
Background Downloads
A foreground service lets a long folder download continue with the screen off, with a notification you can cancel from.
💾
Storage (Android 8 and 9 only)
To save maps in the Downloads folder on older phones. Android 10 and newer need no storage permission.
About the maps: Mouza Plot & Measure BD is an independent app, not made by or connected with the Government of Bangladesh or any land office. The maps are publicly available scans of government Mouza maps prepared by the Directorate of Land Records and Surveys (DLRS). Measurements from a scanned map are estimates and do not replace an official survey, a certified map or your land documents.

Official sources: Directorate of Land Records and Surveys (DLRS): dlrs.gov.bd · official Mouza maps and khatian, to search and order: dlrms.land.gov.bd · Ministry of Land, land information: land.gov.bd
