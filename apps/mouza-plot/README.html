📘 About

Buying or checking land in Bangladesh usually starts with the Mouza map: the survey sheet that shows every plot (দাগ) in a mouza. Finding the right sheet, and then working out a plot's size from it, has meant a visit to an office, a surveyor, or a computer with CAD software.

Mouza Plot & Measure BD puts both steps on your phone. Browse the scanned Mouza maps by division, district and upazila, preview a sheet before downloading it, and measure any plot on it: the area in decimal (শতক), katha, bigha and acre, each side's length, and the perimeter.

The app works in English and Bengali, needs no account, shows no ads and keeps your plots on your phone.

🧭 User Guide

The app has two tabs at the bottom: My maps, where you measure, and Download, where you find maps. The gear at the top right of My maps opens Settings (language, version and this privacy policy).

1. Get a map

  1. Open the Download tab and pick a division, then the district, upazila and survey (CS, SA, RS, BS…). The path you have taken is shown at the top; tap any part of it to go back.
  2. Type in the Filter box to find a mouza or map quickly. Numbers work in English or Bengali digits (১০ finds 10).
  3. Tap a map to preview it. The whole map is fetched to show it, with a progress line such as "Loading preview… 3.2 / 7.5 MiB". Tap the picture to see it full screen and zoom in; use ‹ and › to step through the maps in the folder.
  4. Tap Download file to save it. A map you have previewed is copied, not fetched a second time.
  5. To save a whole folder, tap Download folder… (or long-press a folder). The app first counts the files and their size and asks before downloading. Downloads continue in the background with a progress notification you can cancel from. Maps you already have are skipped, so running it again finishes an interrupted download.
  6. Maps are saved in your phone's Downloads / Mouza Plot folder, arranged by division, district and upazila. The clock icon at the top right lists the maps you have downloaded; tap Measure on any of them to open it for measuring.
On mobile data? A folder can be hundreds of megabytes. The app warns you and offers Wait for Wi-Fi, which starts the download once you are on Wi-Fi.

2. Open your own map

  1. On My maps, tap Open map image and choose a scanned map: PDF, JPG or PNG. You can also open a map from WhatsApp, e-mail or a file manager with Open with or Share and choose Mouza Plot.
  2. For a PDF, page 1 is prepared at the scan's full resolution, which takes a moment for a large sheet.
  3. Rename or delete a map from the list with the pencil and bin icons.

3. Set the scale (calibration)

Areas are only as good as the scale, so check it once for each map. Tap the ruler icon at the top of the map.

  1. A. Printed scale + scan DPI. Most Mouza maps are drawn at 16 inches = 1 mile (1:3,960). When the file records its scan resolution, the app sets this up by itself and tells you so.
  2. B. Two points, known distance. Better when the scan may have been resized or the paper has shrunk. Choose Mark two points, put the crosshair on each end of the printed chain scale bar (or any distance you know) and add a point at each end, then enter the real distance.
  3. Check it with the Distance tool: measure the scale bar and compare.
  4. Under Local units you can set the size of a katha (default 720 sq ft) and a bigha (default 20 katha) if your area uses different values.

4. Measure a plot

  1. Choose the Plot tool and zoom in on the plot (pinch, or double-tap).
  2. Move the map so the red crosshair sits exactly on a corner, then tap Add point. Your finger never covers the line you are tracing.
  3. Go round the plot corner by corner. The dark-blue outline, each side's length and the area update as you go. With Snap on, a corner close to one of another plot jumps to it exactly, so neighbouring plots share their boundary.
  4. Tap Finish. The plot's area appears in decimal, square feet, square metres, acre, katha and bigha, with the perimeter in feet, metres and chains, and every side's length.
Made a mistake? Undo and Redo at the top of the screen reverse any change, including a deleted corner or plot. If an outline crosses itself the app warns you, because its area would not be meaningful.

5. Name and edit plots

  1. Tap inside a plot to select it, then Rename to give it its dag number or a name.
  2. To move a corner, tap its white dot, move the map until the crosshair is where the corner belongs, and tap Place here. While you move it, the app shows what the area would become. Add after inserts a new corner after the selected one.
  3. Delete plot removes it (Undo brings it back).

6. Reports and export

  1. PDF report: select a plot and tap Share PDF to send it by WhatsApp, e-mail or Drive, or use the ⋮ menu › Export PDF report… to save one. It lists each plot's area, sides and perimeter, and the calibration used.
  2. DXF: ⋮ menu › Export DXF… saves the plots for AutoCAD and other CAD software (units: ground inches, the same as the official Mouza DWG drawings).
  3. Validation: ⋮ menu › Validation compares your plots with areas you already know, for example from a deed or from AutoCAD, and shows the difference.

7. Accuracy tips

  1. Calibrate with the chain scale bar on the sheet, not only the printed scale: old paper shrinks and scans get resized.
  2. Zoom in until a printed line is several pixels thick and place each corner on the middle of the line.
  3. A scanned map is a copy of a hand-drawn sheet. Expect small differences from the recorded area, and use the app for checking and planning, not as a legal survey.

❓ Questions

"Preview unavailable: the map server has a problem right now"

The server that hosts the scanned maps sometimes fails for a while. The app already retries; wait a few minutes and tap Try again. Maps you have already previewed or downloaded still open.

I can't find my mouza.

The list shows what the map server holds, which does not yet cover every mouza or every survey. Try the Filter box with both English and Bengali spellings, and look under each survey (CS, SA, RS, BS).

The area is different from my khatian or deed.

First check the calibration against the chain scale bar with the Distance tool. Small differences are normal for a scanned hand-drawn map. A large one usually means the scale is off, a corner is on the wrong line, or the plot's boundary on the map differs from the recorded one.

Where are my downloaded maps?

In the phone's Downloads folder, under Mouza Plot, arranged by division, district and upazila. They stay there even if you uninstall the app.

How do I switch to Bengali?

My maps › gear icon › Language › বাংলা. Numbers switch to Bengali digits too.

⚙️ Technical Overview

  • Platform: Native Android, Kotlin with Jetpack Compose
  • Minimum version: Android 8.0 (API 26)
  • Package: com.rafiqctgbd.mouzaplot
  • Large maps: a 400 DPI Mouza sheet is over 120 megapixels, so it is shown as an overview plus only the region you are looking at, decoded at screen resolution
  • Measurement: plots are stored in scan pixels and converted to ground units only for display, so zooming can never change a measurement; checked against AutoCAD on a reference sheet
  • Downloads: WorkManager foreground jobs, one at a time, with retries for temporary server errors; saved through Android's MediaStore
  • Brand colour: field green, distinct from NextCue's cyan, Docuary's violet and Medicine & Health Reminder's blue
