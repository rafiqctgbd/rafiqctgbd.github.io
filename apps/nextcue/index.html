What It Does

Never forget
anything important

Built for the things people actually forget — bills, renewals, subscriptions, and dates that only come around once a year.

🎯

68 Ready-Made Templates

Instead of a blank form, pick from 68 presets across seven buckets — Bills, Personal Events, Home & Lifestyle, Subscriptions, Renewals, Maintenance, and Health. Tap one, choose a date, done. No typing required.

📅

Today View

Your default screen groups everything as Today, Tomorrow, This Week, and Later — matching how people actually think about what needs doing, instead of a flat list you have to scroll and scan.

⏳

Smart Renewal Reminders

For passports, licences, and insurance, set the expiry date once. NextCue automatically schedules a series of lead-time alerts before it — 6 months, 3 months, 1 month, 1 week out — instead of a blunt yearly repeat that arrives too late to act on.

⏰

Alarms That Actually Fire

Full-screen alerts with Done and Snooze, built on the same production-hardened alarm engine as Medicine & Health Reminder. Exact-time scheduling, automatic snooze if unattended, and automatic restore after your phone restarts.

🔄

Flexible Recurrence

One-time, daily, weekly, monthly, or yearly — plus swipe-to-done and swipe-to-snooze right from the list, each with an Undo option if you swipe by mistake.

📊

History & Undo

Every Done, Snooze, and Missed action is logged in a dedicated History tab — and any entry can be restored with a single tap if you marked something by mistake.

☁️

Optional Google Drive Backup

Sign in with your own Google account to back up your cues and history to your personal Drive. Restore on a new phone, back up automatically each week, or delete the backup any time. Entirely optional — nothing is uploaded unless you sign in.

🛡️

Reminder Reliability Panel

Live status checks for notifications, exact alarms, battery optimization, and full-screen alerts — each with a one-tap fix. Includes guidance for manufacturer battery managers that silently delay alarms.

🔔

Tones & Silent Mode

Four alarm tones to choose from, adjustable snooze duration, and a fully silent mode for vibration-only reminders when you need quiet.

📤

Share Your List

Send your full reminder list via WhatsApp, SMS, or email in one tap — useful for keeping a spouse or family member in the loop on shared household bills.

Your Data, Your Drive

Optional Google
Drive backup

Switch phones, lose a phone, or have one stolen — your cues don't have to go with it.

NextCue works completely offline and needs no account. But if you want your reminders to survive a phone change, you can sign in with your own Google account and back them up to your own Google Drive — under a visible folder called NextCue Backups, where you can see, download, or delete the file yourself at any time.

How backup handles your data →
  • Completely optional — off until you sign in
  • Backs up to your own Drive, never to a developer server
  • Restore everything on a new phone in seconds
  • Optional automatic weekly backup
  • Optional backup reminder if you prefer doing it manually
  • Delete your backup from Drive any time, in-app
  • NextCue can only see the one file it created — nothing else in your Drive
